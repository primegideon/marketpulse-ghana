General Project Roadmap

A high-level, conceptual guide designed to reinforce and validate your data science learning path.

Phase 1: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

  • Descriptive Statistics: Getting a general mathematical snapshot of the data's central tendency, distribution, and volume.
  • Data Distributions: Analyzing the spread and frequency across key variables.
  • Data Visualization: Plotting the data visually using charts to instinctively spot overall trends, structural shapes, and isolated anomalies.
  • Outlier Detection: Identifying and investigating anomalies and extreme values in the dataset.
  • Quality Control: Actively auditing the dataset to identify gaps, missing entries, or corrupted values that require handling.
  • Duplicate and Consistency Checks: Ensuring data integrity by finding repeated records or illogical entries.
  • Correlation Analysis: Examining how different variables interact with one another to discover natural relationships and identify duplicate information.
  • Insights and Patterns: Documenting key findings that could influence the ML approach.

Phase 2: Preprocessing & Feature Engineering

  • Data Cleaning: Fixing the flaws discovered during EDA by filling or removing gaps and normalizing inconsistent measurements.
  • Feature Engineering: Reformatting complex columns (like dates) and creating new, smarter variables that expose critical underlying patterns to the algorithm.
  • Feature Relevance & Selection: Filtering and selecting the most impactful variables based on the findings from EDA.
  • Data Transformation: Converting categorical text labels into math-friendly numerical formats and scaling variables so they share a uniform range.
  • Data Splitting: Dividing the dataset chronologically or structurally into a training set for learning and a separate test set reserved as a final evaluation.

Phase 3: Model Selection & Training

  • Model Selection: Picking the specific family of machine learning algorithms that matches your predictive goals (e.g., choosing regression for continuous numerical targets).
  • Model Training: Allowing the chosen algorithm to process the training dataset so it can learn the mathematical rules connecting inputs to the final answer.

Phase 4: Evaluation

  • Performance Scoring: Running the trained model on the hidden test data to grade its real-world predictive accuracy.
  • Error Analysis: Calculating baseline metrics to see exactly how far off the predictions are on average and checking where the model makes its largest mistakes.

Phase 5: Post-Evaluation & Application

  • Optimization: Twisting the model's internal hyperparameter knobs or adding more contextual features if the initial evaluation score needs improvement.
  • Interpretation: Querying the finalized model to rank feature importance and extract clear, data-driven insights about what variables drive the system.
  • Monitoring & Maintenance: Putting the model to work for future forecasting while continuously tracking it over time to ensure its accuracy does not decay as real-world conditions shift.
